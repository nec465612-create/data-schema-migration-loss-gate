APPROVED PRODUCT/DESIGN SCOPE
Data Schema Migration Loss Gate is a public evidence workstation for one narrow question: does an explicit mapping from a declared old schema to a declared new schema preserve information?

Design the complete first-time judge journey:
1. Explain the question, trust boundary, public-data warning and exact-material disclaimer before wallet or chain interaction.
2. Discover and explicitly choose only actually detected MetaMask, OKX Wallet or Rabby providers.
3. Show configured network and wallet state accurately.
4. Create or open a schema case.
5. Compare old and new schema definitions.
6. Submit and freeze the mapper's explicit mapping and defaults.
7. Evaluate the frozen case and show LOSSLESS, LOSS_FOUND or UNRESOLVED with factual phase/revision/result evidence.
8. Make retained transaction context, export, reconciliation, quarantine and authoritative readback understandable when those states occur.

Make these actors legible in the public UI: schema owner, mapper and evaluator. Explain GenLayer's role accurately as independent evaluation/consensus before the stored outcome; do not exaggerate or claim verification of external facts.
